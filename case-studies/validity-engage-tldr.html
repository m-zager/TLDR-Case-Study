The Problem

Email marketers track a few competitors but can't see how their industry is shifting or how their program stacks up. Validity had 25+ years of email data (2.5B+ data points across 30,000+ brands) that could answer those questions, but no AI product to put it in marketers' hands. Engage, a new agentic AI platform, was that product. I designed its Market Intelligence agent from 0 to 1 in 6 months.

My Responsibilities
  • Lead product designer for the Market Intelligence agent
  • Ran a two-day assumption-mapping workshop with developers, data scientists, and a PM to align on the MVP
  • Validated product-market fit with leadership, SDRs, AMs, and beta users
  • Built live-coded prototypes with Claude + VS Code and helped standardize a design system shared by three agents
Key Considerations & Trade-offs
  • Value on first view. Featured Insights are the first thing users see, so I grounded them in what marketers care about (industry trends and competitor benchmarks) and what the LLM could pull reliably (6 scored categories, top emails, head-to-head comparisons).
  • A core UX loop. Insight card → insight page → AskEngage follow-up questions. The loop gives users depth and also drives credit consumption, the business model.
  • Charts cut from the MVP. Data visualization and rankings would have added significant data and dev work, so we shipped email creatives plus AskEngage first to stay focused on the core vision.
  • Time to value over self-serve setup. We configured each user's company and tracked brands on the back end at first, then built the front-end setup later.
  • Prototyping in code. It sped up iteration and got stronger stakeholder buy-in, but made work harder to see and hand off. A living branch-history page and a branch naming convention fixed the visibility.
Improvements

Feedback from leadership, sales, and beta users showed that insights skewed quantitative, didn't separate easy wins from strategic findings, padded out a fixed set of six cards, and hid the emails behind them. The next iteration:

  • Replaced the required metric with a dynamic label, heading, and subheader so insights could cover qualitative shifts like launches and repositioning
  • Added brand logos and email creative thumbnails to invite exploration
  • Removed the "AI-gradient" styling to reduce clutter
  • Swapped prompt pills for an explore + bookmark footer that opens the insight page with an AskEngage response already started
Outcome
  • Launched three core features (Featured Insights, Custom Insights, and Signal Feed) that save marketers hours of competitive research
  • The core UX loop became the pattern for all three Engage agents and for AskEngage across the app
  • My card and layout exploration shaped Engage's visual language and design system
  • Next: make insights more actionable, deliver them in Slack or Claude, and track credit use, time to first insight, and AskEngage engagement
