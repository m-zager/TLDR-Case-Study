The Problem

In 2023, Tailwind launched Tailwind Ads so its e-commerce users could create and manage AI-generated social ads across networks. After the beta launch, too few users were setting their first ad live. My job was to find where they got stuck and raise the conversion rate to a first live ad.

My Responsibilities
  • Product designer on Tailwind Ads improvements (2023, 3 months), working with a PM and a user researcher
  • Interviewed beta users to find pain points in onboarding
  • Used Indicative to find where users dropped off in the journey
  • Ran a workshop with the product team, then designed and tested improvements iteratively
Key Considerations & Trade-offs
  • Designing for first-time advertisers. Users were very small business owners who run everything themselves. Most had never run a paid ad campaign, so every step needed to explain what's required and what happens next.
  • Prioritize by pain and data. An opportunity map and a customer journey map, paired with Indicative drop-off data, narrowed the work to three problems: low confidence, ads that didn't feel tailored to the business, and confusion about charges.
  • Reuse over reinvent. The ad editor borrowed the layout of QuickScheduler, which users already knew, instead of introducing a new flow. That saved design and dev time.
  • Show progress without overload. Users needed to see what to do next without a wall of information, so the stepper has five steps, the first already complete after onboarding, with hover states for detail.
Improvements
  • Added a stepper to the Draft Ads page, plus quick links to resources about Tailwind Ads
  • Turned the empty dashboard into a guide: the metrics to expect, knowledge base articles, and a path to reviewing draft ads
  • Redesigned ad review with a live preview, a tooltip explaining each ad's purpose, network icons, and the option to set a single ad live from the editor
  • Prefilled ad copy that's easy to edit, with a Regenerate Ad Copy button
  • Reworked the service fee modal with a clearer visual hierarchy, so users can see the charges and ad summary at a glance before going live
Outcome
  • Shipped improvements across onboarding, ad review, and the service fee step
  • Next: bring Tailwind Ads into other parts of the app to reach more of the user base and lift engagement and conversion
