The Problem

Keywords decide whether Pinterest Pins reach the right audience at the right time. Discovery showed Tailwind users were doing keyword research outside the app, in tools like Pinterest Trends and PinClicks. They kept what they found in spreadsheets, Notion, or Google Drive, then pasted it back into Tailwind's scheduler. The goal was to bring the whole workflow (find, save, recall, and use keywords) into Tailwind to save users time and keep them in-app.

My Responsibilities
  • Product designer for Tailwind's keyword research tool, from discovery through MVP (3 months)
  • Helped run user interviews to learn how users find and use keywords
  • Partnered with a PM on scope and a three-part user flow: the core experience, keywords in the Pin Scheduler, and generating Pin details from a keyword
  • Designed, prototyped, and ran usability tests, iterating until feedback turned positive
Key Considerations & Trade-offs
  • Core experience first. The feature would eventually reach many parts of Tailwind, so I focused early designs on search, find, save, and manage, built to scale to other touchpoints later.
  • Match how users already work. Users added about three keywords per Ghostwriter generation and cared most that at least one made it into the output, which shaped how saved keywords feed Pin titles and descriptions.
  • Learn fast. I built a foundational prototype quickly and shared async video walkthroughs so the team could give feedback while I kept designing.
  • Generation cut from the MVP. Dropping the flow for generating Pin details from keywords let the team ship about a month sooner. The dashboard alone solved the core pain point of research happening outside Tailwind.
Improvements

Usability tests surfaced three sources of confusion: testers missed the tool's full value and didn't know where the data came from, didn't understand why they'd pair a URL with a saved keyword, and wanted clearer metrics and faster saving. The next iterations:

  • Added an inline banner explaining where Tailwind's keyword data comes from, plus empty states, quick links, and resources that show more of the tool's value
  • Reworded URL pairing to explain that it makes saved keywords available across Tailwind, and showed where to find them
  • Added multi-select so users can save several keywords at once and act on them right away
  • Made metrics like the Tailwind score easier to understand and improved search
Outcome
  • Shipped the keyword research dashboard, bringing a workflow users did outside Tailwind into the app
  • Cutting generation from the MVP saved about a month of development time
  • Next: bring keyword research into the Pin Scheduler and AI-driven workflows, prioritized by what users do
